# Music sources

Every track here is human-made music released under **CC0** (public domain: free for commercial
use, no attribution required) on [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org), published between 2010
and 2020, and none of the pages mentions AI. Some are dual-licensed (each alongside CC0) and are
used under CC0. Each was converted unedited (apart from the format) to Ogg Vorbis with EBU R128
loudness normalization to -16 LUFS (the masters are in the repository's music_masters/), then
re-encoded at quality 0 for this package. It comes with danny.playerversus_purgatory (the PURGATORY mode).
